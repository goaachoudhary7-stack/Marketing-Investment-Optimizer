Marketing Investment Optimizer

An interactive Power BI analytics project designed to help marketing leaders understand campaign performance, identify inefficient spending, and allocate budgets toward the highest-performing channels.

Business Problem

Marketing teams often invest across multiple channels without a clear view of which campaigns generate the strongest returns.

This project answers key business questions:

Which channels generate the highest revenue and ROAS?
Where is marketing spend being underutilized?
Which campaigns have the lowest customer acquisition cost?
How efficiently does each channel convert investment into revenue?
Where should the business increase, reduce, or reallocate its budget?

Project Objective

The objective was to transform campaign-level marketing data into an interactive decision-support dashboard that enables stakeholders to:

Monitor marketing spend, revenue, conversions, and profitability
Compare performance across campaigns and channels
Identify high-spend, low-return investments
Track funnel performance and open opportunities
Make evidence-based budget allocation decisions

KPI Definitions
Blended ROAS = Total Revenue ÷ Total Marketing Spend

Blended CPA = Total Marketing Spend ÷ Total Conversions

Efficiency Index = Revenue Share ÷ Spend Share

An Efficiency Index greater than 1.0 indicates that a channel generates a higher share of revenue than its share of marketing spend.

Dashboard Pages

1. Executive Overview

Provides leadership with a high-level view of overall marketing performance through KPI cards, trends, channel comparisons, and interactive filters.

2. Campaign and Channel Performance
Evaluates campaign effectiveness using spend, revenue, conversions, ROAS, CPA, and efficiency metrics.

3. Channel and Funnel Analysis
Tracks opportunities across funnel stages to identify conversion gaps, pipeline bottlenecks, and channels contributing the
strongest commercial outcomes.

Business Insights Generated

The dashboard helps decision-makers identify:

Channels producing strong revenue relative to their spend
Campaigns consuming budget without delivering sufficient returns
Differences between spend contribution and revenue contribution
Funnel stages where opportunities are being lost
Campaigns that should be scaled, optimized, or reviewed
Potential areas for marketing budget reallocation

Business Recommendations

Increase investment in channels with consistently strong ROAS and an Efficiency Index above 1.0.
Review campaigns with high spending, high CPA, and weak revenue contribution.
Protect high-converting campaigns before expanding their budgets.
Investigate funnel stages with a high concentration of stalled opportunities.
Evaluate performance using both revenue and efficiency metrics—not spend or lead volume alone.
Reassess budget allocation regularly as campaign performance changes.
